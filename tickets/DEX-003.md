# DEX-003 - Data-First Uniswap V2 Survivorship Substrate

**Priority:** P0
**Status:** AWAITING_REVIEW
**Next required actor:** Sol 5.6 High
**Dependencies:** DEX-002 (ACCEPTED), DATA-012 PairCreated ingestion substrate, ADR-0015
**Layer:** acquisition / canonical data / point-in-time universe
**Architecture:** `docs/adr/0015-data-first-dex-research-substrate.md`

## Objective

Build the first research-authoritative DEX dataset before any DEX factor or experiment:
a complete Ethereum Uniswap V2 factory census, complete historical Swap/Sync event
coverage for every direct USDC/USDT pair, canonical daily pool observations, and
point-in-time active/inactive membership without survivorship filtering.

## Required Domain

- Ethereum mainnet only.
- Canonical Uniswap V2 factory only.
- Every PairCreated event from deployment block 10,000,835 through a pinned finalized
  cutoff.
- Every pair where exactly one token is an accepted USDC or USDT quote.
- Current web-API visibility or liquidity must not determine inclusion.

## Scope

1. Extend the existing raw JSON-RPC acquisition pattern to Pair `Swap` and `Sync`
   events, block headers, and token-decimal calls.
2. Preserve requests, responses, receipts, block identities, and raw dependencies.
3. Prove Swap/Sync completeness through two independent RPC providers per block chunk
   or canonical receipt-level reconstruction. One successful `eth_getLogs` response is
   insufficient.
4. Publish the four immutable ADR-0015 products: `dex_pool_registry`,
   `dex_pool_events`, `dex_pool_daily`, and `dex_universe_daily`.
5. Freeze base/stable-quote orientation and retain event time, conservative
   `source_available_at`, and actual `retrieved_at` separately.
6. Derive daily `ACTIVE`, `INACTIVE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, and `REVIVED` states using only
   lagged historical observations. Never delete a pool or infer inactivity from missing
   acquisition evidence.
7. Seal calendar year 2025 as structural holdout data. Quality checks may inspect
   schema, hashes, counts, and gaps, but no factor, return, ranking, or portfolio output.
8. Publish `research/sprint_004/51_DEX_SURVIVORSHIP.json` with census counts, block
   coverage, source reconciliation, event coverage by pool, active/inactive/revived
   histories, unresolved gaps, dataset IDs, and catalog reconciliation.

## Blocking Gates

1. PairCreated coverage is contiguous and reconciles the complete factory census.
2. Every selected pair has complete, reconciled Swap/Sync coverage from creation to
   cutoff; any unresolved range blocks PASS.
3. Token identity, decimals, orientation, OHLC, volume, reserves, and liquidity
   reconstruct deterministically from retained evidence.
4. Known inactive and revived pools reconstruct correctly at historical dates.
5. Decisions at time `t` cannot observe events unavailable at `t` or same-day closes.
6. Current API snapshots do not control historical membership or canonical prices.

## Out of Scope

- Factor design, parameter selection, backtests, or experiment execution.
- Other DEX protocols or chains.
- GeckoTerminal, DexScreener, DefiLlama, or Birdeye as historical authority.
- DEX execution costs, PAPER, promotion, or LIVE.
