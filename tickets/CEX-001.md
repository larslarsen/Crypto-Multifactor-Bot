## Objective

Deliver the first actually usable CEX research spine: a historically complete Binance
USD-M linear-perpetual contract family with aligned price, trade/order-book
microstructure, OI, funding, liquidation, basis, and execution-cost data; immutable raw
lineage; a pinned release descriptor; and a clean consumer harness that proves causal
execution mechanics.

This is not a 20/50/100-ticker ticket. Acquisition covers every contract in the declared
historical venue family. It is not complete when only a parser, sample, report, row count,
daily bar panel, or subset works.

## Non-negotiable domain

- Venue/domain: Binance USD-M linear perpetuals.
- Universe: every perpetual contract listed at any time in the declared source coverage,
  including delisted/renamed contracts, derived from versioned historical evidence.
- Spot/index references: same venue where available and explicitly identified.
- Frequency: trades at source granularity; bars, BBO, and fixed-depth state at one minute
  or finer for the executable research layer.
- Missingness: typed `UNAVAILABLE`/gap state, never zero-fill.
- Time: event, source availability, retrieval, and revision times remain distinct.
- No current-listing, current-liquidity, fixed-N, or hand-selected acquisition filter.

## Milestones and blocking gates

### Gate 0 — quarantine false authority

Preserve all five BitMEX parquet files and manifests, but atomically mark their catalog
authority rejected/superseded so neither latest resolution nor any experiment can consume
them. The operation must be idempotent, resumable, auditable, and tested against exact IDs.
Publish a quarantine report containing pre/post catalog rows, file hashes, row counts,
nonzero/null/empty-interval counts, and resolver results. Do not rewrite the parquets.

The BitMEX normalizer/backfill must fail on a missing rate, non-finite rate, empty required
interval, all-zero nontrivial history, source-field drift, fetch error, or incomplete
requested coverage. It may not be reused as Binance authority.

### Gate 1 — source, procurement, and full-universe authority

Using real source objects, establish:

- complete historical contract inventory and version semantics;
- exact product/date/instrument coverage for official sources and any licensed source;
- source incident/revision and availability-time semantics;
- licensing/retention rights, quoted external cost, download volume, compressed and
  working storage, and deterministic acquisition method; and
- real non-empty early/middle/recent samples, including a delisted contract and more than
  one liquidity regime, for every required product.

Official Binance data is authoritative where complete and checksummed. Historical
L2/BBO, liquidation, and OI may use a licensed capture source only after overlap with
official trades/funding/marks reconciles. The owner must authorize any purchase after the
quote is recorded. Failure to meet this gate blocks the ticket; it does not authorize a
smaller universe or synthetic substitute.

### Gate 2 — reference and raw acquisition

Publish bitemporal contract versions with native symbol, canonical identity, linear style,
margin/settlement asset, multiplier, quantity/price units, tick/step sizes,
listing/delisting/effective times, and raw lineage. Acquire all accepted source objects
content-addressably with request/download receipts, checksum/byte verification, revision
identity, bounded retries, durable per-object progress, and deterministic resume.

A second run over completed coverage must issue no unnecessary download, create no
duplicate economic object, and reproduce the same inventory. Interrupted downloads cannot
be promoted. Current exchange metadata cannot overwrite historical versions.

### Gate 3 — normalized products

Publish every required product separately. Each row binds native symbol to the applicable
contract version and retains source lineage. Required economic semantics include:

- trade side/aggressor, price, native/base/quote quantities, trade ID, and duplicate state;
- BBO spread, top-N depth by side, imbalance, sequence continuity, and typed book gaps;
- native contract OI, multiplier, base quantity, USD notional, and conversion price/time;
- realized versus indicative funding, exact funding event, interval, sign convention,
  cap/floor when available, and long/short cashflow convention;
- liquidation event identity, liquidated-position side, price, native/base/USD quantity,
  and duplicate state;
- mark/index/spot identities and causally computed basis; and
- effective-dated maker/taker fees plus explicit spread/impact inputs.

No table passes solely because it is non-empty. Constant, zero, null, impossible,
duplicate, truncated, unit-ambiguous, or gap-obscuring fields fail the relevant quality
gate.

### Gate 4 — independent reconciliation and coverage

- Reconcile official and licensed trades, funding, marks, and contract coverage on
  declared overlap intervals.
- Reconstruct one-minute OHLCV from raw trades and compare the economically comparable
  fields with provider bars across instruments and regimes.
- Validate book sequencing/snapshots and emit every discontinuity as a gap.
- Independently recompute OI and liquidation USD notional on retained examples across
  contract versions.
- Prove funding sign/cashflow for long and short positions using retained real events.
- Produce per-instrument/product first/last times, expected/observed counts, gaps,
  duplicates, revisions, and cross-product intersection.
- Require manifest hash, file byte, parquet row, catalog row, and report reconciliation.

Unresolved coverage is not silently dropped. The bundle may declare a usable intersection
only when every exclusion is explicit and the acquisition universe remains fully reported.

### Gate 5 — bundle, release, and consumer harness

Publish a versioned bundle descriptor with exact dataset/manifest IDs, schemas, code and
config identities, mappings, coverage/gaps, intersection counts, and semantic API version.
Build a clean wheel/sdist from a clean commit and prove installation in an empty
environment. The wheel must contain the required reference, universe, market, validation,
portfolio/accounting, and consumer modules.

The bulk/as-of reader must reject missing, empty, stale, schema-incompatible,
unreconciled, or wrong-bundle inputs. The harness must prove:

- `source_available_at <= decision_time < entry_time < exit_time`;
- strictly later executable price/quote selection;
- explicit funding, fees, spread/impact, margin, liquidation, and position-unit
  accounting;
- deterministic run identity and resume; and
- byte/row-equivalent results between uninterrupted and interrupted/resumed runs.

Use a declared development interval only. Do not emit factor payoff summaries, inspect a
sealed holdout, or make PAPER/LIVE claims in this ticket.
