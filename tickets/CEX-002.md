## Source contract

### Official Binance primary sources

- Public Data archive inventories for historical-name discovery and selected checksummed
  one-hour USD-M klines, mark-price klines, index-price klines, premium-index klines,
  five-minute metrics, funding events, and the bounded cost sample.
- Official USD-M REST history for realized funding and contract metadata where available.

Historical `trades`, `aggTrades`, and complete `bookTicker`/`bookDepth` families are not
acquisition inputs. For duplicate daily/monthly packaging, select one representation per
economic interval: valid monthly objects for completed months and daily objects only for
uncovered dates. Discovery listings do not imply acquisition.

### Coinalyze secondary source

- Free daily Binance-perpetual liquidation history, preserving separate long and short
  values and source units.
- Daily OI, funding, and OHLCV overlap used only to reconcile the secondary source before
  accepting its liquidation aggregates.
- The API key is read only from `COINALYZE_API_KEY`, redacted from URLs, receipts, logs,
  exceptions, and reports, and never committed.

Binance liquidation observations after 2021-04-27 are explicitly censored by the venue's
latest-per-symbol-per-second publication rule. The product name, schema, metadata, quality
report, and consumer API must say `observed`, never `complete`, `all`, or equivalent. A
missing source interval is `UNAVAILABLE`, never zero liquidations.

## Universe and temporal contract

- Discover membership from the union of all relevant official archive families, not a
  current exchange-info response or one product prefix.
- Cover every historically observed USD-M perpetual, including delisted instruments, and
  report products that did not exist for part of a contract's life.
- Retain venue-native symbol, canonical identity, source event/period time,
  `source_available_at` when known, retrieval time, raw object/request identity, schema,
  code/config identity, and quality state.
- Unknown publication time remains unknown; it is not copied from event time.
- Full-history daily alignment is mandatory across price, OI, realized funding, basis,
  and observed liquidation. Native one-hour/five-minute/event products remain separate.
- Explicit product gaps do not erase historical membership. A Coinalyze non-mapping is a
  liquidation/intersection gap, never a zero and never a reason to reduce acquisition.

## Milestones and blocking gates

### Gate 0 — quarantine false authority

Atomically quarantine the five exact invalid BitMEX funding artifacts without rewriting
their files. Reauthenticate exact catalog rows, manifest identities, bytes, and economic
counts on every replay; require compare-and-swap catalog state and default resolver
exclusion. Correct the normalizer's missing-to-zero and unconditional-PASS behavior.

### Gate 1 — full free-source qualification

Against real sources, paginate complete discovery listings, descend the selected `1h`
interval partitions, distinguish headed/headerless formats, discover the full historical
perpetual set, retain early/middle/recent/delisted samples, and build an exact
non-overlapping acquisition manifest without extrapolating from sample symbols.

Storage preflight separately includes selected compressed raw bytes, the exact frozen
first/midpoint/last per-contract book-ticker/depth sample, a conservative normalized/catalog
bound from real samples, atomic temporary high-water space, and an operating reserve. It
does not count unselected ticks, books, or overlapping archive packages.

The revised qualification run reports selected raw/cost-sample bytes and the largest
selected compressed object exactly. Normalized/catalog, normalization temporary high-water,
operating reserve, and total sufficiency remain `unknown` until a later bounded sizing step;
unknown never passes Gate 2.

Qualify real Coinalyze Binance symbols and non-empty daily liquidation/OI/funding/price
samples. Record API retention, units, attribution/license terms, first/last observations,
and overlap-reconciliation rules. No download beyond bounded qualification begins until
the reviewer accepts the source matrix.

### Gate 2 — raw acquisition and deterministic resume

Acquire all selected official objects content-addressably with checksums, revision
identity, durable per-object progress, bounded retries, and interrupted-download safety.
Persist redacted Coinalyze request/response receipts and content hashes. A rerun over
completed coverage performs no unnecessary download and creates no duplicate economic
object.

Pin the prospective holdout boundary. A live stream collector is deferred to a later
ticket after historical tradability is established.

### Gate 3 — normalization and economic quality

Publish every required product separately with exact raw lineage. Prove the native kline
total/taker-buy volume semantics and derived hourly taker imbalance; preserve OI stock
versus change and units;
separate realized/indicative funding and prove long/short cashflow; compute basis causally;
preserve liquidation side convention and censorship; and publish fee/spread/depth cost
inputs without inventing unavailable history.

Non-empty alone never passes. Constant, all-zero, all-null, truncated, duplicate,
unit-ambiguous, impossible, gap-obscuring, or mislabeled fields fail.

### Gate 4 — reconciliation and coverage

- On bounded early/middle/recent samples, aggregate official one-minute klines and compare
  comparable OHLCV/taker-volume fields with selected native hourly klines. This does not
  authorize full one-minute acquisition.
- Reconcile Coinalyze daily price, OI, and funding with official Binance observations on
  declared overlap before accepting its liquidation series.
- Independently recompute retained OI notional, basis, funding cashflows, taker flow, and
  liquidation imbalance examples.
- Produce per-instrument/product first/last times, expected/observed counts, gaps,
  duplicates, revisions, and the full daily intersection.
- Reconcile archive checksums, raw bytes, normalized rows, manifests, catalog rows, gap
  tables, and reports.

No unresolved coverage is silently dropped. A usable daily intersection may exclude a
contract/date only when the gap is explicit and the acquisition universe remains fully
reported.

### Gate 5 — release and clean consumer check

Publish a versioned bundle descriptor pinning every dataset/manifest/schema/mapping,
source/code/config identity, coverage/gap product, unit convention, censorship flag, and
intersection count. In a clean environment, load the pinned bars and required
custom derivatives products into a NautilusTrader catalog and read them back with exact
row/time/value reconciliation. This is a data-consumer check, not a backtest or model.

Prove interrupted/resumed acquisition and publication produce byte/row-equivalent release
identities. Readers reject missing, empty, stale, incompatible, unreconciled, or wrong-
bundle inputs.
